❓ Teacher Guide

What is this app?

A weekly vocabulary system for Gemara class: you create blocks of words, generate timed tests, and students take them on their Chromebooks through a shared link with an access code.

1 · Blocks & Students

Each block is one week of vocabulary. Click + בלוק חדש, give it a number, a title and the week dates. Select a block to manage its words and tests.

In the 👥 תלמידים card, add your class roster (name + optional nickname). When a roster exists, students simply tap their name at the start of a test instead of typing it.

2 · Words

  • Add one word: use the + הוספת מילה tab.
  • Bulk upload: paste many words at once, one per line, in this format:
    Hebrew | shoresh/milah | English
  • You can edit ✏️ or delete 🗑 any word later from the words table.

3 · Tests

  • Click ✨ יצירת מבחן, pick the words, the timer per word, and the direction — Hebrew→English, English→Hebrew (a virtual Hebrew keyboard is provided), or Mixed (each question flips randomly).
  • Choose how the printed score card shows the grade: fraction (8/10), percent (80%), or both.
  • Save as draft — students cannot open it yet. Start now — the test goes live immediately.
  • Every test has a 4-digit access code shown on its card. Write it on the board — students must type it before starting. This stops anyone without the code from taking the test.
  • Click 📋 to copy the student link and share it (email, Google Classroom, etc.).
  • When the lesson is over, click ⏹ סיום to close the test — students can no longer submit. You can reopen it later.

4 · Results, Grading & Score Cards

  • Students do not see their grade — they see "Your teacher will give you your grade." You review first.
  • The 📊 תוצאות tab shows every submission with the class average. Click a row to review it: toggle any ✅/❌ to override a grade (e.g. accept a typo), then click ✓ אישור סופי.
  • 🖨 הדפסת כרטיסי ציון prints handout cards (6 per page, ב״ה header) for all finalized results, using the score format you chose.
  • 📊 תצוגת כיתה (top of the page, when a test is live) opens a live board that refreshes every 10 seconds — great for projecting. It also shows the access code.
